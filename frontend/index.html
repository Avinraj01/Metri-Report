Standardized 5-Point Eccentricity Testing (Cl. A.4.7).

Under OIML R-76-1:2006 Clause A.4.7, multi-support weighing instruments undergo rigorous off-center loading evaluations at ⅓ Max load across the central reference point and four quarter-quadrants. Metri-Report automatically calculates turning point errors (ΔE = |Ei - Ecenter| ≤ MPE), validating corner deviation tolerances with cryptographic certainty.

ΔE ≤ 0.001g (PASS)

Repeatability & Tare Balancing Verification (Cl. A.4.10 & A.4.6).

Under OIML R-76-1:2006 Clause A.4.10, non-automatic weighing instruments undergo consecutive independent load applications at ~0.5 Max and ~0.8 Max with 3 sequential runs each. Metri-Report computes standard deviation (σ), maximum range deviation (|Emax - Emin| ≤ |MPE|), and tare balancing accuracy automatically.
